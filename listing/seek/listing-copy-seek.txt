ETSY DESCRIPTION
Meet Pico — and go looking for treasure with him.

Pico's Seek & Color is a printable find-it coloring book for kids ages 4–8. Thirty original pages. Thick easy lines. One big scene per page. On every page Pico hides in a new forest moment — and a few things are waiting to be found.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Look at the picture.
2. Find the things (3, then 5, then 6 — it gets a little braver).
3. Color the whole scene.

WHAT YOU GET
• US Letter PDF (8.5 × 11 in)
• A4 PDF
• Cover, “this book belongs to”, how-to-play, 30 seek-and-color pages, and a Seeker Champion certificate
• Instant download. Print the whole book or one page at a time.

WHY KIDS STAY
Finding is a tiny hunt. Coloring is the prize. Pico’s face is on every page, so the book feels like one adventure, not a pile of random animals.

HOW TO PRINT
Print in actual size (100%), portrait, single-sided. Crayons and colored pencils are perfect. If you use markers, slip a spare sheet underneath.

This is a digital item. No physical book will be shipped.
Original art. No licensed characters.
