Pico's Forest Friends — print instructions

1. Use the Letter PDF in the US / Canada, the A4 PDF everywhere else.
2. Print at 100% (Actual Size). Do not "fit to page".
3. Portrait. Single-sided.
4. Crayons and colored pencils are best on normal printer paper.
5. Markers: put a spare sheet under the page you are coloring.
6. Heavier paper (120–160 gsm / 32 lb) feels more like a real book.
