ABOUT YOUR SHOP  (723 / 5000 characters)
================================================
PicoKids makes original printable coloring books for children ages 4–8.

Our first book, Pico’s Forest Friends, follows a little fox cub through a quiet wood — treehouses, picnics, puddles, and a birthday cake. Thirty pages. Thick lines. One big scene per page. Made for small hands, crayons, and a slow afternoon.

Every download includes US Letter and A4, so families can print at home on the paper they already have. No licensed characters. No tiny corners that make a four-year-old give up.

PicoKids is a small independent shop. We care about pages that feel like a real book, not a pile of random animals. If a child can recognize Pico from the first page to the last, we did our job.

Thank you for printing with us.
