PicoKids Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 September 2026

PicoKids is a small independent shop on Etsy. We sell original printable coloring books as digital PDF downloads. This policy explains what information we see, what we do with it, and how you can ask us about it.

1. Who is responsible

The shop “PicoKids” on Etsy is the seller. We are based in Romania (European Union).

Etsy, Inc. runs the marketplace, your Etsy account, checkout, and payments. Etsy’s own privacy policy covers how Etsy uses that data. This page only covers what PicoKids does as the seller.

Contact us through Etsy Messages to the PicoKids shop. That is the right place for privacy questions about an order.

2. What we collect

We do not run our own website checkout and we do not ask children for information.

When you buy from PicoKids on Etsy, we can see the order information Etsy shares with sellers, which typically includes:

• Your Etsy account name
• The email address Etsy uses for the order
• The item you bought, price, date, and order number
• Messages you send us on Etsy
• Download / delivery status for the digital file

We do not collect payment card numbers. Etsy and its payment partners process payments.

We do not create extra mailing lists, we do not sell emails, and we do not track you off Etsy.

3. Children

Our coloring books are made for children ages 4–8, but the customer is the parent, caregiver, or teacher who buys the file.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Please do not send us a child’s full name, school, address, or photos. If a “this book belongs to” page is filled in after you print, that copy stays in your home — we never see it.

4. How we use order information

We use it only to:

• Deliver the PDF (Etsy instant download)
• Answer your messages
• Fix a broken file or a missing download
• Meet bookkeeping and tax rules that apply to us in Romania / the EU
• Handle a dispute if one is opened on Etsy

Legal bases under the GDPR: performing the contract (your order), legal obligation (invoices and tax), and legitimate interests (customer support and fraud prevention).

5. How long we keep it

Order records stay as long as Etsy keeps them available to sellers and as long as tax law in Romania requires (generally several years for invoices). We do not keep a separate marketing database.

6. Sharing

We do not sell or rent your information.

It is processed by Etsy as the platform. If the law requires it, or if we must defend a legal claim, we may disclose what we have. We do not send your data to advertisers.

7. Where data is processed

Etsy is a global platform. Your account data may be processed in the United States and other countries under Etsy’s terms. As an EU seller we still handle shop messages and orders under the GDPR.

8. Your rights (EEA / UK and similar laws)

You can ask us, via Etsy Messages, to:

• Access the order information we have about you
• Correct it
• Delete it, where the law allows (we may keep invoices if tax rules require)
• Restrict or object to certain uses
• Receive a copy of what we hold

You can also manage your Etsy account privacy in Etsy settings, and download your Etsy data from Etsy.

If you are in the EU/EEA you may complain to your local data protection authority. In Romania that is the ANSPDCP (dataprotection.ro).

9. Cookies

PicoKids does not set its own cookies. Etsy.com uses cookies as described in Etsy’s policies.

10. Files you download

The PDF is a coloring book for personal use at home, in class, or as a gift. You may print it as many times as you like for your family or classroom.

You may not resell the PDF, share the file publicly, upload it to other shops, or claim the drawings as your own. The characters (including Pico) and the pages remain PicoKids original artwork.

11. Changes

If this policy changes in a real way, we will update the date at the top and publish the new version here.

Thank you for printing with PicoKids.
